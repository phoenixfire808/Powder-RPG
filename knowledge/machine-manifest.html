Foundry Manifest

Everything that can be built, smelted or forged, read straight out of the running game. Stations are real gates: a lit furnace only works while fire is actually burning in its chamber, an anvil wants iron bars, and a workbench has to be placed and stood beside.

Pulled from the live game state (R.RECIPES and R.ITEMS), so this is what your session can actually build right now. Station names match the craft menu. Right-click a placed machine in game to open its panel: it lists inputs, outputs and the one action it is waiting on.
