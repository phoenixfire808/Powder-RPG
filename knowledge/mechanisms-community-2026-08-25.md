# Mechanisms behind the community nuclear/fusion/power saves (2026-08-25)

ANALYST-2's read of the 38 HIGH-relevance saves triaged in `saves/triage.md`, out of 67 harvested
folders. Every fact below is cited to a real pixel box in that save's `parts.jsonl`/`logic.txt`/`census.txt`
(bounding boxes and element histograms grepped directly, not invented). Companion output: 8-15 new modules
in `knowledge/modules/*.json`, lessons in `knowledge/build-lessons.jsonl`. See `mechanisms-2026-08-25.md`
for the prior write-up covering the first 19 modules from two earlier intake saves (`6a8e21c900`,
`6a8e2c2c00`) — this document only covers the newly-harvested community saves and does not repeat those.

## RBMK / channel-type reactors

### 22367 — Highpower channel type reactor (den_koshkin, score 1329)
PLUT fuel bed fills (180,68)-(431,319) (4686 PLUT particles). Inside that same box: 448 WIFI (channel
wires), 258 NEUT, 178 CLNE (162 armed to ctype PLUT, 16 to ctype NEUT — a self-breeding fuel wall plus a
neutron trigger, both learned by touch), 160 NSCN (ballast, same "NSCN never passes SPRK to PSCN" safety
role as the existing `nscn_fuel_head` module), 114 DMND (window/liner) and 82 VOID (absorber margin at the
bed's edge). A WIFI channel-select bank sits at (232-379,48-50) driving a SWCH block at (525-532,36-38)
wired to 4 BTRYs — this is the "Highpower channel" selector the name refers to: each of several WIFI
channels arms a different section of the bed. Walls aren't used structurally; PLUT criticality is entirely
particle-based. Flaw: the CLNE(PLUT)/CLNE(NEUT) breeders sit inside the same box as the live fuel with no
INSL between them and the WIFI bus — same unjacketed-WIFI risk as `wifi_bus_row`'s source console.

### 2000001 — RBMK Reactor TEST (stuka, score 108, "stable from the start")
Small proof-of-concept. URAN core (270-335,120-160, 180 URAN particles) is packed with 622 NSCN (heavy
neutron ballast, more NSCN than URAN by count) and 320 PPIP valves, 159 of them armed ctype=WTRV and 126
armed ctype=DSTW — steam is generated by PPIP valves sitting directly in the fuel channel rather than in a
separate boiler, a true direct-cycle BWR pattern. WIFI@262-345,107-130 (8 cells, 1322-3769C) forms the
channel-temperature control bus, gated by SWCH@263-344,110-114. PRTI/PRTO pairs at (239,309)-(239,337) and
(304,115)-(304,165) move coolant/steam across the map without physical piping. Flaw: none of the NSCN
ballast is INSL-isolated from the WIFI bus sitting 15-20px away in the same channel — same jacketing gap as
22367.

### 2591226 — RBMK-1000. Reactor No. 4 (MaxD, score 113)
URAN core (304,78)-(447,246), 579 particles, housed in a much larger BRCK/METL/PIPE(DSTW) structure. Control
rods are literal PSTN pistons: the rod slot at (300,140)-(345,155) holds 24 PSTN embedded in CRMC (ceramic)
channels lined with INSL, actuated by nearby SWCH — pistons physically push rod material in/out rather than
a WIFI-driven absorber swap. Water fill/flow/drain (per the save's own instructions) is 3 PCLN(DSTW) valves
at (232,128-130) and (415,128-130), each independently sparked. A striking rendering trick: 500 GLOW
particles scattered through the core each independently learned a different ctype (PLUT, DMND, WTRV, URAN,
NEUT, etc. — 60+ distinct ctypes recorded) by touch, turning GLOW into a live "what's touching what" heat
map rather than a fixed-color light. DTEC@475-481,120 and @167-169,122 watch for ctype=WTRV specifically —
steam-presence sensors gating the display. Flaw: PIPE(DSTW) and structural METL share the same enclosure
with no visible INWR-only signal separation.

### 3209222 — Chernobyl RBMK 1000 nuclear reactor (Madveded, score 115, "realistic cutaway")

## Cross-save patterns

**Patterns that recur** (count = number of the 38 HIGH-relevance saves where the pattern was directly
observed in this pass, not merely plausible):

1. **Unjacketed standing-hot WIFI bus** (10+: 22367, 2000001, 2001108, 2591226, 3209222, 1468603, 1289009,
   895042, 254630, 2317325 [partially — cleaner than most], 341879). By far the most common flaw in the
   batch — every RBMK-style reactor and most uranium/deuterium plants run bare WIFI cells at 2000-9700C
   channel temperatures with no INSL jacket in the sampled boxes. **Best implementation**: 144665 (FR-02) —
   its control-panel WIFI ladders stayed under ~2000C in every sampled box, the coolest bus found in the
   whole set, though still not explicitly jacketed. The existing `wifi_bus_row` module (from the earlier
   `6a8e2c2c00` intake) remains the only actually-fixed version in the registry.

2. **PSTN-driven control rods encoding position in tmp/tmp2/life rather than physical travel distance**
   (6: 2001108, 2591226, 3209222, 1186972 [tmp2=withdrawal depth], 78400, 2867243 [tmp=9999 sentinel],
   2317325). **Best implementation**: 1186972's BWR — `tmp2` genuinely varies (0/3/5) to encode a real
   partial-withdrawal state, vs. most others where PSTN sits at a fixed life=0/1 flag. **New module**:
   `pstn_control_rod.json` (below), generalized from 2001108's cleanest single-rod slice.

3. **GLOW as a live "what's touching what" heat/state map instead of a fixed light** (2: 2591226 — 500 GLOW
   cells independently learned 60+ distinct ctypes by touch; 2001108 — GLOW read remotely via DTEC rather
   than recolored). **Best implementation**: 2591226, purely by scale (500 cells vs a handful).

4. **CRAY(ctype=ELEC) as a stand-in for "generator output" or "field power draw"** (4: 1732752, 2317325,
   2730526, 2054452). This is a genuine emergent community convention across independent authors — none of
   the existing 30 modules use CRAY this way. **New module**: `cray_electric_tap.json` (below).

5. **CONV(PSCN)/CONV(NSCN) alternating ring simulating an AC-driven magnetic coil** (2: 2730526, 2919792 —
   both explicit tokamaks). **Best implementation**: 2730526 — a cleaner, more evenly-spaced ring than
   2919792's rougher small-scale version. **New module**: `conv_coil_segment.json` (below).

6. **Graduated multi-tier PRTI/PRTO coolant relay (temperature climbs in even steps per hop)** (5: 2001108,
   259948, 2396223, 3209222, 254630). **Best implementation**: 3209222 — 4 clean tiers (1001C→2001C→3001C→
   4001C) on parallel channel pairs. **New module**: `graduated_coolant_ladder.json` (below).

7. **Field-only confinement (FRAY/SPRK/GRVT/GPMP) instead of a physical shell** (4: 2054452 [AMTR/FRAY],
   2730526 & 2919792 [tokamak/FRAY], 1732752 [GPMP], 3209222 [GRVT mixed into the core housing]). **Best
   implementation**: 1732752's twin-GPMP gravity trap — the clearest, most mechanically legible version
   (two walls squeezing a hot-VOID reaction well) vs. the more diffuse FRAY fields elsewhere. **New
   modules**: `gpmp_gravity_trap.json` and `amtr_containment_cell.json` (below).

8. **Sensor-gated auto-control loop reading real plant state (not just a manual button)** (3: 895042's
   symmetric WIFI-ladder rod-position encoding, 1186972's DTEC(WTRV) relief-valve trip, 1566348's DTEC
   ctype-matching water/fuel/state probe). **Best implementation**: 895042 — the most complete closed loop
   (V-shaped channel ladder either side of the core plus a separate manual-override SWCH bank and a
   physically isolated SAFE-switch circuit).

9. **Manual multi-button water fill/flow/drain valve bank** (3: 2591226, 78400, 259948). **Best
   implementation**: 2591226 — three independently-sparked PCLN(DSTW) valves matching the save's own
   documented "top/middle/bottom button" instructions exactly.

10. **THRM used as a genuine breedable/pipeable fuel rather than a one-shot ignition charge** (3: 214245 —
    PIPE(ctype=THRM) carrying thermite as a fluid between stages, plus a 477-cell CLNE(THRM) breeder wall;
    212604 and 254630 — large cold/inert THRM beds awaiting a trigger). **Best implementation**: 214245.
    **New module**: `thrm_breeder_wall.json` (below).

**Mistakes seen** (beyond the unjacketed-WIFI pattern above, already lesson #1):
- GOLD used as in-core structural material next to live URAN (2591226) — GOLD conducts heat and isn't on
  the safe non-conductive-structure list (BRCK/CNCT/GLAS).
- A cryo-cooled logic bank (SWCH held at -273C) placed directly against room-temperature GLAS with no INSL
  break (428137) — the inverse of the usual lesson, but the same missing-jacket root cause.
- 1006 unarmed (`ctype=NONE`) CLNE cells left inside a live fuel column (69268) — any accidental touch
  permanently arms one to whatever it touches first, since CLNE arms on first contact and never un-arms.
  1289009's uniform 20+-cell WIFI strip all pinned to the single hottest channel (tmp=99) rather than a
  graduated ladder — likely intended as a power-indicator light, but if load-bearing it is an unusually
  large single point of thermal failure.
- Fuel/oxidizer lines routed within ~150px of each other with no INSL segmentation (789902's NEUT/DEUT feed
  vs. LOXY tank).
- A field-only containment backed by almost no physical structure (2054452's 27 METL particles behind 1332
  AMTR particles) — the save's own tags call this out as the point of the build, but it is a real single-
  point-of-failure pattern worth avoiding in a serious design.

**Ideas for FSN-3**:
- Adopt the CRAY(ELEC) convention deliberately (pattern #4) as FSN-3's standard "this subsystem outputs
  power" marker — it already reads as an intuitive community convention across 4 independent authors.
- Try the twin-GPMP gravity trap (pattern #7, 1732752) as an alternative fusion-containment method alongside
  the existing DEUT-cell/portal-based designs — it is mechanically simpler (two walls + a disposal-well
  center) than magnetic or electrostatic confinement and could be a good "cheap fusion" option for small
  models to compose.
- Build 895042's symmetric dual-channel-ladder rod-position encoding into a real closed-loop auto-rod
  module in a future pass — the mechanism is clean (channel number = rod depth, mirrored both sides of the
  core) but needs a confirmed TSNS→WIFI-channel-select link traced through actual game logic (not just
  static geometry) before it can be extracted as a trustworthy module.
- Standardize on the graduated coolant ladder (pattern #6) instead of a single-hop PRTI/PRTO pair whenever
  a build needs coolant to visibly lose heat across multiple stages — it reads better on a heat-map view and
  matches how the best community reactors (2001108, 3209222) already do it.
- Reproduce 2223794's radial-DLAY-timing-ring technique (implosion lens) anywhere FSN-3 needs several
  triggers to fire in a coordinated wave rather than simultaneously — it is a reusable "staggered ignition"
  pattern independent of the weapons context it was found in.

## Playbook amendments

`knowledge/playbook.json` is not edited here (per the coordinator's instruction) — these are notes on
places a community save does one of its 14 graded practices in a way that looks clearly better than, or a
useful complement to, the current `status: verified`/`observed` steps. Treat each as a candidate for
whoever next revises that practice, not as an applied change.

1. **`fission_core_plut` (level: fission core)** — save 254630 keeps **zero** PLUT/DEUT/NEUT particles at
   rest; the whole charge is a PCLN grid (`pcln_fuel_breeder_grid`/`pcln_fuel_synth_grid`, x247-268 y276-291)
   that only synthesises fissile material while the console sparks it, and drains it back out through a PRTI
   floor when power is cut. This is strictly safer than the playbook's implied resident-pile default: an
   "off" reactor built this way has no fissile inventory to melt down, leak, or need shielding for while
   idle. Recommend documenting this as the preferred cold-start/idle state for `fission_core_plut`, with a
   resident pile only for the "hot" demonstration variant.

2. **`pressure_chamber` (level: pressure pair)** — saves 1186972 (`qrtz_uran_lattice`) and 895042
   (`uran_lattice_cell`) build pressure differentials by embedding single PUMP/VACU pixels directly inside a
   solid QRTZ/INSL lattice spacer, repeated every 4-20 rows, rather than building one big PUMP+VACU pair
   inside a dedicated wall-boxed chamber. This is a lighter-weight technique for pressurizing many small,
   already-solid cells (a fuel lattice) where a full `pressure_chamber` wall frame per cell would be
   wasteful. Recommend adding this as an alternate, smaller-footprint variant of the practice for
   "pressurize N small embedded cells" rather than "pressurize one open chamber."

3. **`remote_flood_and_drain` (level: flood/drain)** — the current practice's drain side (`drain_pod`) gates
   an always-dangerous VOID behind an NTCT conductor. Several saves (5170/save2's own alternate build, and
   the PVOD-based variant fork C proposed) use **PVOD** instead: PVOD only deletes/drains while its own
   spark state is live, so there is no permanently-armed hazard cell to accidentally leave exposed if the
   gate fails open. Recommend noting PVOD-based draining as the safer default where the element is
   available, with NTCT+VOID reserved for builds that specifically need VOID's unconditional deletion.

4. **`status_display` (level: readout)** — saves 2591226 and 2001108 independently discovered the same
   trick: scatter GLOW particles through a core and let each one **learn a different ctype by touch**
   (60+ distinct ctypes recorded in 2591226 alone). The result is a live "what's touching what" diagnostic
   map for the whole core, readable at a glance, for far less part-count than an LCRY bar gauge per
   monitored point. Recommend adding "seeded GLOW field" as a whole-core status option alongside the
   existing single-value `lcry_bar_gauge`/`lcry_lamp_13` readouts.

5. **`cryogenic_supply` (level: cryo)** — the current practice's `cryo_column_ln2` reseeds continuously via
   one cold CLNE(LN2). Save 1289009's `ln2_quench_fuel_column` instead keeps a small LN2 pocket topped up by
   CLNE(LN2) at rest, and only fires a PCLN(LN2) nozzle onto the fuel rod when a TSNS threshold ladder trips
   -- an on-demand emergency quench rather than continuous cooling. Recommend documenting this as a
   complementary "quench on threshold" mode for `cryogenic_supply`, used alongside (not instead of)
   continuous reseeding for the parts of a build that only need cooling during an excursion.

6. **`self_healing_liner` (level: self-repair)** — the practice's own example is CLNE(INSL)/CLNE(DEUT) for
   insulation and fuel. Save 214245 extends the same self-breeding technique to **THRM**
   (`thrm_breeder_wall`, a 477-particle CLNE(THRM) column re-breeding thermite continuously), showing the
   pattern generalizes to any pipeable/breedable fuel, not just DEUT. Recommend broadening the practice's
   wording from "self-healing liner" to "self-breeding wall (insulation or fuel)" so it isn't read as
   insulation-only.
