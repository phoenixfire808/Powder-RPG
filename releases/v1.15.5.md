# Release v1.15.5 — Deaths counter in the always-on HUD

## Summary

The death counter was readable from exactly two places: the inventory panel (rarely open
unless you're mid-crafting) and the Esc menu (only after you specifically think to look).
Neither was visible at the moment of respawn — which is the exact moment a player most
often wants to see how many times they've died so far this session. v1.15.5 wires the
counter into the always-on HUD, top-right corner, gated on `deaths > 0` so it stays out
of the way for fresh sessions and pops in the instant your first death is recorded.

## Changes

- **Death counter now shows in the always-on HUD** (top-right, only when deaths > 0):
  "Deaths: N" in bright orange. Was previously only visible in the inventory panel and
  Esc menu, easy to miss in the moment after a respawn when you actually want to see it.

## Affected files

- `scripts/lua/rpg.lua` — added the Deaths line to the always-on HUD draw loop
  (top-right corner, only renders when `R.deaths > 0` so the HUD stays clean for players
  who haven't died yet). No plugin changes needed; the HUD is the canonical place every
  other top-right readout lives, and the existing `R.deaths` counter (already incremented
  in the death handler) is the source of truth.

## Migration notes

None. Pure QoL / visibility improvement. No save-format change, no behavior change, no
config change, no breaking change for plugins. The existing `R.deaths` counter continues
to increment exactly as it did before; this just makes it visible at-a-glance in the HUD
in addition to the existing inventory / Esc-menu views.

## How to verify

1. Build or update to v1.15.5.
2. Start a fresh world — confirm the HUD does NOT show a Deaths line yet (it's gated on
   `deaths > 0`; the HUD stays clean for a fresh session).
3. Die (any cause works — fall, fire, CO₂ suffocation, fall damage). The death handler
   increments `R.deaths`; you respawn at the surface.
4. Look at the top-right corner of the HUD: "Deaths: 1" in bright orange should be
   visible immediately on respawn.
5. Die again. The counter reads "Deaths: 2", and so on.
6. Confirm the same counter still shows correctly in the inventory panel (bottom row) and
   in the Esc-menu entry — those existing views are unchanged and continue to work.
