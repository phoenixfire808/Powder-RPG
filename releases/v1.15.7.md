# Release v1.15.7 — companion respawn follow-along + F8 clear-around restored

## Summary

Two small but previously-annoying quality-of-life fixes that share the v1.15.7 umbrella:

1. **F1: companion follows the player through `R.spawnPlayer()`** — dying deep and
   respawning at the surface used to leave your companion stranded at the death site
   (or worse, dead herself), because `R.spawnPlayer()` only moved the player. Now it
   also teleports the companion to the fresh spawn point, gated on "active and not
   already dead" so it doesn't fight the auto-respawn path.
2. **F8: `R.clearAround()` re-added** as a no-arg version that clears a 6-wide × 11-tall
   box above the spawn point — matching the v2 shape. The function had been lost in
   the v3 rewrite (commit `6c8d889`), and the visible "blood pool at the spawn point"
   bleed noticed at the round-3078 respawn is what that loss was actually costing. Now
   the air right above where you respawn is genuinely fresh — no leftover fire, no
   lingering CO₂, no BLD pool.

## Changes

- **F1 — Companion respawn follow-along.** `R.spawnPlayer()` now teleports the
  companion to the fresh player spawn point. Previously `companion.lua:R.COMP.x/y`
  were never reset, so dying deep and respawning at the surface left Aster stranded at
  the death site (or worse, dead). Gated on `C.active` and not `C.dead` so it doesn't
  interfere with the `REVIVE_DELAY` auto-respawn path. Magic Mirror behavior is
  preserved (the save/restore around the call still works, since the teleport only
  fires when the companion gate conditions are met).
- **F8 — `R.clearAround()` re-added as a no-arg version.** Reads `R.P.x` / `R.P.y`
  and clears a 6-wide × 11-tall box above the spawn point — the same shape the v2
  build had. Both call sites (`generateWorld` line 840, `R.spawnPlayer` line 893)
  were already passing no args and would have crashed on the missing function. This
  brings it back; the visible "BLD pool at the spawn point" bleed noticed at the
  round-3078 respawn is now actually fixed (the prior cosmetic-only fix only stopped
  re-spawning more blood; the original clearing logic still wasn't running).
- **Magic Mirror + REVIVE_DELAY paths unaffected.** `R.spawnPlayer()` keeps the same
  signature, the same HP save/restore if called by the accessory, and the same
  per-spawn-state reset block added in v1.15.4. The two changes here compose on top
  of those rather than overriding them.

## Affected files

- `scripts/lua/rpg.lua` — two changes:
  - F1: extended `R.spawnPlayer()` (around line 893) with a companion-teleport block
    gated on `R.companion and R.companion.active and not R.companion.dead`.
  - F8: re-added `R.clearAround()` (no args) clearing a 6×11 box above `R.P.x` /
    `R.P.y`. Both call sites (`generateWorld` line 840, `R.spawnPlayer` line 893)
    already passed no args; the function adds the actual clearing logic.
- `scripts/lua/rpg_plugins/companion.lua` — **read-only** at v1.15.7 time
  (per release notes this round confirms the gate is on `companion.lua`'s exported
  `R.COMP` table; the actual teleport writes `R.COMP.x` / `R.COMP.y`). No edits.

## Migration notes

None. Pure QoL / consistency fixes on existing functions. No save-format change, no
config change, no behavior change for callers beyond the two specific bugs being
fixed. `R.spawnPlayer()` continues to do what its name says; `R.clearAround()`
continues to be the no-arg function both internal call sites have been calling since
the rewrite. No breaking changes for plugins.

## How to verify

**F1 verification (companion follow-along):**

1. Build or update to v1.15.7.
2. Spawn a world, let the companion join you, walk deep underground (so she's
   actively following at distance).
3. Die. Respawn at the surface.
4. Before this fix: Aster was stranded at the death site (or dead). After this
   fix: Aster is standing right next to you at the surface spawn point, alive,
   active, gate respected.
5. *Negative test*: trigger the auto-respawn path (`REVIVE_DELAY` ticks past zero
   while she has zero HP). Confirm the gate on `C.dead` skips the teleport, so the
   auto-respawn revives her in place rather than yanking her to the player. Both
   paths work without fighting each other.

**F8 verification (clear-around re-add):**

1. Build or update to v1.15.7.
2. Spawn a world, generate a foreground mess around your start position: shoot
   some fire, drop some BLD (blood), generate a small cloud of noxious gas.
3. Press **F8** — the no-arg call. (Or press **R** to respawn, which internally
   calls the same function.)
4. Before this fix: nothing visibly changed (function was missing, calls were
   silently no-op'ing). After this fix: a 6-wide × 11-tall rectangular region
   directly above the spawn point is empty air — no fire, no blood, no gas.
   Stand on the cleared region and confirm the air around you reads clean on
   the hover tooltip.
5. Round-trip: die to fire underground, press R to respawn at the surface.
   Confirm the spawn region (not just your immediate tile) is clean — confirming
   that the v1.15.4 reset states (`R.o2 = 100`, etc.) AND this v1.15.7 spatial
   clear both fire.
