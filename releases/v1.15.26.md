# v1.15.26 — vehicles + ventilation fan + survival food loop

**Feature batch.** Three parallel tracks ship together: minecart/rail/mine-lift kits on the
workbench tree (`@vehicles`), a powered ventilation fan that registers into the ambient gas
scrubber system plus a CO2-scrubber fix (`@machines`), and confirmation of the minimal
plant/food survival loop with idempotent hot-reload wrappers (`@survival` pillar 0b).

## Summary

- **Verdict:** New gameplay systems (vehicles, life-support machine) + survival QoL
  (food loop confirmed, hot-reload guard). No native rebuild.
- **Scope:** `scripts/lua/rpg_plugins/vehicles.lua`, `scripts/lua/rpg_plugins/machines.lua`,
  `scripts/lua/rpg_plugins/survival.lua`, and `scripts/lua/rpg.lua` (V-key brush vs board
  gate). Follows the v1.15.8 CONVENTION (plugin work rolls into the next rpg.lua bump with a
  unified CHANGELOG entry).
- **Behaviour delta:** craft rail segments (LMB-drag snapped track), place minecart, V to board,
  D/A drive, S brake/dismount; vertical mine lift with grid-powered call buttons; V cycles
  brush shape only when not riding and not within ~26 px of a rideable. VENTFANKIT vents ambient
  CO/CO2 via `R.scrubbers` and pushes CO2/SMKE along its duct; SCRUBBERKIT now also registers
  into `R.scrubbers` when powered. Till SOIL, plant SEED near WATR, harvest WHEAT, bake BREAD,
  right-click `R.FOODS` to eat; `R._survival` guard prevents stacked illness/bed wraps on
  hot-reload.
- **Risk:** medium for vehicles (new mount/ride physics path, rail graph persistence); low for
  ventfan/scrubber (additive scrubber registry, existing particle-kill paths unchanged); low for
  survival (wrapper guard only, food loop already live).

## Changes

Bullets pulled verbatim from `R.CHANGELOG[1]` at `scripts/lua/rpg.lua:207-211`:

- Vehicles (@vehicles): minecart + rail + mine-lift kits on the workbench/anvil tree. Rail kit:
  LMB-drag snapped track (flat / 45° / vertical shaft). Minecart: place on track, V to board,
  D/A drive, S brake/dismount via R.mount/R.ride. Mine lift: vertical shaft cage with
  grid-powered call buttons. V only steals brush-shape when riding or next to a vehicle.
- Ventilation fan (VENTFANKIT): new powered life-support machine - registers into R.scrubbers to
  pull down ambient CO/CO2 near you and pushes real CO2/smoke along its duct. CO2 scrubber
  (SCRUBBERKIT) now also registers into R.scrubbers when powered (was only killing particles,
  not reducing R.gas.co/co2). Recipe unlocks at 10W tier alongside air pump and scrubber.
- survival.lua (@survival pillar 0b): minimal plant/food loop confirmed live — till SOIL (GOO
  recipe), plant SEED on patch with nearby WATR, harvest WHEAT, bake BREAD at furnace,
  right-click any R.FOODS item to eat and refill R.need.food. Idempotent R.eat/R.spawnPlayer
  wrappers (R._survival guard) so hot-reload no longer stacks illness/bed wraps.

## Migration notes

- **Plugin authors:** if you wrap core functions (`R.eat`, `R.spawnPlayer`, etc.), use an
  `R._yourPlugin` guard table the same way `survival.lua` uses `R._survival` — hot-reload
  without a guard stacks monkey-patches and can recurse.
- **Save compatibility:** rail segments and placed vehicles persist via existing machine/item
  save paths; no save-format version bump. Older saves without vehicle state load forward with
  empty rail graphs.
- **No native rebuild.** Lua-only change; hot-reload (`R.hotReloadRequested`) is sufficient
  once `R.VERSION` reads `1.15.26`.
- **Awaiting live confirmation:** bridge-verified on port 9876 (`R.VERSION=="1.15.26`,
  `pluginStatus.vehicles==ok`, `pluginStatus.machines==ok`, `VENTFANKIT` present,
  survival plug ok). Owner visual/feel confirm still pending for minecart drive, lift call
  buttons, and ventfan gas reduction in a real cave.

## How-to-verify

1. **Pre-flight.** Confirm the HUD version readout reads `v1.15.26`. Hot-reload or restart if
   older.
2. **Path 1 — rail + minecart.** Unlock 10W tier (or sandbox). Craft `RAILKIT`, LMB-drag track
   on flat ground and a 45° ramp. Craft `MINECARTKIT`, place on rail. Press **V** near cart to
   board. **D/A** to drive, **S** to brake/dismount. Expected: cart follows rail graph, no
   teleport; dismount clears mount state.
3. **Path 2 — mine lift.** Build a vertical rail shaft, place `LIFTKIT`, wire grid power to
   call buttons at both ends. Expected: cage moves on shaft when called; free-falls if power
   lost.
4. **Path 3 — V-key coexistence.** Stand away from vehicles, press **V**. Expected: brush
   shape cycles (square/circle/single). Ride or stand within ~26 px of a rideable, press **V**.
   Expected: boards vehicle instead of changing brush shape.
5. **Path 4 — ventilation fan.** Place powered `VENTFANKIT` in a CO2/smoke pocket (or use
   scrubber). Watch `R.gas.co` / `R.gas.co2` near the player and duct push of CO2/SMKE
   particles. Expected: ambient gas counters drop near powered units; scrubber also registers
   into `R.scrubbers` (not particle-kill only).
6. **Path 5 — food loop.** Till SOIL (GOO recipe), plant SEED on patch with nearby WATR,
   harvest WHEAT, bake BREAD at furnace. Right-click any `R.FOODS` item. Expected:
   `R.need.food` refills; no duplicate illness stacks after hot-reload.
7. **Negative test — hot-reload survival wrappers.** Hot-reload twice with survival plugin
   active. Expected: single illness/bed wrap layer; no stack-overflow or double-drain on eat.
8. **Architecture verification.** Grep `syncScrubbers` in `machines.lua`; grep `R._survival`
   in `survival.lua`; grep vehicle board proximity gate in `rpg.lua` / `vehicles.lua`.
