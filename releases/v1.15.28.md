# v1.15.28 — blood spray + companion chat + HUD + tree veins + liquid/env gradients

**Simulation feel + companion QoL batch.** Rolls up the v1.15.27 core/plugin
fixes (blood, Aster chat, HUD rows, tree water veins) plus the v1.15.28 ambient
physics pass (resting liquids settle naturally; oxygen/pressure/gas stratify with
depth instead of flat meters).

## Summary

- **Verdict:** Gameplay feel + companion QoL + HUD clarity. No native rebuild, no
  save-format change.
- **Scope:** `scripts/lua/rpg.lua` (blood spray, HUD band, tree veins,
  absorbStandingWater, ambient O2/pressure/gas loop), `scripts/lua/rpg_plugins/companion.lua`
  (Enter-chat intent parser + manual-task gating), `scripts/lua/rpg_plugins/survival.lua`
  (warmth/events moved out of HP band). Follows the v1.15.8 CONVENTION.
- **Behaviour delta:** damage spawns vivid outward BLD spray; Enter-chat always
  runs Aster's command parser (even with a model driver connected) with broad
  mine/gather/craft/light/shaft/quest/bring-me fallbacks; top-left HUD uses fixed
  rows (HP / Day / optional air+food / T+P gauges) with version at bottom-left;
  rain on canopy routes down trunk columns before soil soak; resting surface water
  soaks into soil (not tree trunks); open-air O2 and positive pressure nudge at
  the surface with depth-thinning below; H2/GAS rise, CO2 pools.
- **Risk:** low for blood/HUD (visual + spawn only); medium for companion chat
  (new intent surface, manual mode blocks scripted follow); low-medium for
  liquid/env (additive ambient loop, capped OXYG population).

## Changes

Bullets from `R.CHANGELOG[1]` at `scripts/lua/rpg.lua:207-211` (ver 1.15.27),
plus v1.15.28 liquid/env work (ambient loop in `rpg.lua` movePlayer tick, not yet
a separate CHANGELOG head entry at time of writing):

- Blood: damage now spawns a vivid red BLD spray from your character over
  several frames — outward velocity from facing direction, not a tiny upward
  dribble. BLD colour brightened to 0xFF1020.
- Companion: Enter-chat always runs Aster's command parser even when a
  model driver is connected. Broad intent fallbacks (mine/get/gather/craft/light/dig
  shaft/quest help/bring me) — no more silent 'Got it.' Manual tasks block the
  scripted follow brain until the chain finishes.
- HUD: top-left status rows no longer stack AIR/Day/food on the same pixels;
  survival plugin warmth/events moved out of the HP band. Single version readout at
  bottom-left.
- Trees: rain on canopy/trunk routes water down the trunk column before
  soil soak (treeWaterVeinsTick); trunk WOOD no longer instantly absorbs resting
  rain.
- Liquid settle: resting WATR on absorbent ground (GOO/soil) slowly
  soaks in via `absorbStandingWater`; tree trunks/canopy exempt so rain routes
  down veins first. BLD uses real WATR liquid physics and pools naturally.
- Environment gradients: breathable air baseline thins with depth
  (not a flat sealed-room floor); surface OXYG seeds in a tight ground band
  (OXYG_CAP=220) and positive `sim.pressure` nudges open sky cells; H2/GAS
  gravity patched to rise, CO2 sinks; breathing consumes nearby OXYG particles
  and exhales CO2; daylight GRSS photosynthesis converts nearby CO2 to OXYG.

## Affected files

- `scripts/lua/rpg.lua` — BLD element + `bloodSprayLeft` tick; HUD fixed-row band
  (`HUD_X`/`vitalsY`); `treeWaterVeinsTick` + `absorbStandingWater`; ambient O2/
  pressure/gas loop (depth `thin`, sealed penalty, OXYG spawn/release, H2/GAS
  gravity, photosynthesis); version readout at `H-10`.
- `scripts/lua/rpg_plugins/companion.lua` — `R.companionHandleChat`,
  `companionFallbackIntent`, `manualTaskActive`, `MANUAL_TASK_NAMES`; chat hook
  queues to model driver but always parses locally; scripted brain respects
  manual chains.
- `scripts/lua/rpg_plugins/survival.lua` — warmth bar + event banner repositioned
  (no longer drawn inside HP row).

## Migration notes

- **No save-format bump.** Hot-reload (`R.hotReloadRequested` / F9) sufficient once
  `R.VERSION` reads `1.15.28`.
- **Companion driver:** model mode still queues messages for `companion_driver.py`,
  but local intent parsing now runs on every Enter-chat message regardless — driver
  and parser can both act on the same line; manual tasks set `C.mode = "manual"`.
- **Liquid/env:** depth-thinning replaces the old flat sealed-room baseline; shallow
  enclosed rooms should no longer read as instant vacuum.

## How-to-verify

1. **Pre-flight.** HUD bottom-left reads `v1.15.28` (or `v1.15.27` if lead bump
   pending). Hot-reload or restart if older.
2. **Path 1 — blood spray.** Take fall damage or stand in fire. Expected: vivid red
  BLD particles spray outward from the character over ~6 frames, pool as liquid,
  colour 0xFF1020.
3. **Path 2 — companion chat.** With or without model driver: chat "get me iron",
   "dig a shaft", "craft workbench", "light a torch", "what's the quest". Expected:
   concrete reply + task starts; not silent "Got it." Scripted follow pauses until
   chain finishes.
4. **Path 3 — HUD rows.** Play with low O2, low food, and cold (snow biome).
   Expected: HP bar row 1, Day row 2, optional AIR/food row 3, T+P gauges below,
   log tail below gauges — no pixel overlap; version at bottom-left only.
5. **Path 4 — tree veins.** Rain on trees in a new world. Expected: water runs down
   trunk column before soaking soil; resting rain on trunk not instantly absorbed.
6. **Path 5 — liquid settle.** Place/spill WATR on open GOO/soil away from trees.
   Expected: slow soak-in over time; no instant vanish on trunk wood.
7. **Path 6 — env gradients.** Stand at surface vs ~400px deep in open air vs sealed
   shallow room vs deep sealed room. Expected: surface O2 stays high; depth thins
   meter; sealed shallow room not instant vacuum; CO2 pools low, H2 rises high when
   present.
8. **Architecture verification.** Grep `bloodSprayLeft`, `treeWaterVeinsTick`,
  `absorbStandingWater`, `companionHandleChat`, `manualTaskActive` in source.
