# Release v1.15.4 — respawn-doesn't-clean-state fix

## Summary

Dying to anything — CO₂ / suffocation, fire, falls, deep-heat radiation — and respawning at
the surface used to leave you in the *exact same toxic environment that just killed you*.
The polluting gas envelope (CO / CO₂ from the firebox, deep-heat radiation, etc.) stayed
at its death-time concentration, your `R.o2` read 0 from the asphyxiation that killed you,
and your food / water meters were still drained from where you died. A fresh spawn at the
surface would then poison you again within seconds and trigger a second death before you
could even move. This release makes a respawn actually fresh: `R.spawnPlayer()` now resets
every per-spawn state back to a clean baseline.

## Changes

- **`R.spawnPlayer()` (in `scripts/lua/rpg.lua`)** now resets `R.o2 = 100`, `R.gas`,
  `R.need.food` / `R.need.water`, `R.hurt` / `R.bloodLast`, `R.uvAccum` / `R.radAccum`,
  and `R.hp = 100`. After the reset, respawning at the surface gives a clean atmosphere,
  a full HP bar, empty gas envelope, empty radiation / UV meters, and full food / water.
- **Magic Mirror accessory is unaffected** — it saves `R.hp` before calling
  `R.spawnPlayer()` and restores it after, so the new reset logic doesn't clobber the HP
  the player's trying to teleport back with.
- **`R.deaths` counter and inventory-halving** (the existing "you died, inventory halved"
  behavior in the death handler) continue to work unchanged.

## Affected files

- `scripts/lua/rpg.lua` — single change: extended `R.spawnPlayer()` with the new reset block
  (around line 871). No plugin changes needed; the function is the same one the death
  handler, the Esc menu's "Respawn at surface (R)" entry, and the R hotkey all call into.

## Migration notes

None. This is a pure bugfix. No save-format change, no config change, no behavior change
for callers — `R.spawnPlayer()` keeps doing exactly what its name says (spawn the player at
the surface), it now also wipes the transient state that previously carried over from the
death. No breaking changes for plugins: every place that called `R.spawnPlayer()` continues
to work.

## How to verify

1. Build or update to v1.15.4.
2. Get into a position where you'll suffocate — dig straight down until the air thins and
   you start taking CO₂ suffocation damage.
3. Die. Then press **R** (or Esc → Respawn at surface) to respawn at the surface.
4. Before this fix: the surface immediately read "carbon dioxide build-up" / "you cannot
   breathe" and HP drained again within a couple of seconds. After this fix: clean
   atmosphere, full HP, full O₂, full food / water — your surroundings are genuinely a
   fresh start. The same test passes whether the death cause was CO₂, fire, deep-heat
   radiation, or fall damage.
5. Magic Mirror smoke test: wear a Magic Mirror accessory, die far from the surface, press
   **X**. Before this fix: same carry-over bug. After this fix: HP at the value you had
   before dying (the accessory saves / restores around the call), everything else clean.
